TimerMask privacy information

Publisher: Adeba Studio. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Information stored on your device

TimerMask stores timer routines, appearance preferences, language and accessibility settings in your browser's local storage. TimerMask does not currently require an account, and the app's own code does not transmit your saved routines to Adeba Studio servers.

Backup files

If you choose Export backup, TimerMask creates a JSON file on your device. Import reads the file you select and, after confirmation, replaces the routines and sequence stored in this browser.

Offline functionality

TimerMask uses a service worker and browser cache to make app files available offline. You can clear this data through your browser settings.

External services

Links to YouTube open a separate website with its own privacy practices. Hosting infrastructure may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own policies. TimerMask does not currently load third-party advertising or payment scripts.

Control and deletion

You can delete saved TimerMask data by clearing this site's browser storage. Export any routines you want to keep first.

Contact and future changes

Adeba Studio must add its public support contact address here before publication. If advertising, analytics, donations or payments are enabled, this notice must be reviewed and updated first.
