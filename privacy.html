Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · Applies to the TimerMask website/PWA and the TimerMask Android app.

In short

TimerMask does not require an account, and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal data on any server. Your timers, saved presets and settings stay on your own device. The free Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may process some device data as described below.

Data stored on your device

TimerMask saves your preferences (language, colours, sound settings), your custom timers, saved presets/routines, and the state of a running timer, using your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device through TimerMask. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Android app)

The free Android version shows one banner ad on the main screen, never while a timer is running or in fullscreen. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and ad interactions in order to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This processing is controlled by Google; see how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) TimerMask asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can review your choice any time in Settings → Install & About → Ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android system settings. Buying the ad-free upgrade removes the banner.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. TimerMask never sees or stores your payment details. The app only learns, through Google Play, whether the purchase has been made on your Google account so that it can hide ads. There are no subscriptions.

Notifications and permissions

The Android app may ask to send notifications so it can alert you when a timer phase changes while the screen is off. It may also request permission to schedule exact alarms for the same reason. These alerts are created and shown locally on your device. The website/PWA uses no permissions other than optionally keeping the screen awake while a timer runs.

Website / PWA

The TimerMask website shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking cookies, and does not load third-party scripts. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and operation.

Children

TimerMask is a general-purpose timer and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to export or erase on our side; your local data is under your control on your device. For data processed by Google (ads, purchases), use your Google account's privacy tools. If you are in the EEA/UK you also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.
