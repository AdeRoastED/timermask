# Play Console → Data safety (suggested answers; verify against AdMob's current guidance)

* Does your app collect or share user data? **Yes** (only because of AdMob).
* Data is encrypted in transit: **Yes**. Users can request deletion: **N/A – no data is stored by you on servers**.
* Collected & shared (via Google AdMob, purpose: **Advertising or marketing**, optional: no):
  * **Device or other IDs** (Advertising ID)
  * **Approximate location** (derived from IP) – only if AdMob reports it; select as shown in the AdMob Data-disclosure page
  * **App activity → App interactions**, **App info and performance → Diagnostics / Crash logs**
* Purchase history: processed by Google Play – nothing to declare for you.
* Not collected: name, e-mail, precise location, contacts, photos, audio, health data, financial info.
* Ads declaration: **Contains ads = Yes**. Target audience: 13+/18+. Advertising ID declaration: **Yes, used for advertising**.
